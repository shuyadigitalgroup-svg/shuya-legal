Provision and performance of the contractual service (creation and management of the account, publication of Listings, activation of the Pass, issuing of receipts and handling of refund requests): processing based on the performance of the contract concluded with the User (General Terms of Use).

Moderation, platform security and fraud prevention (prior verification of Listings, handling of reports, logging of administration validations and protection against unauthorised access): processing based on SHUYA’s legitimate interest in ensuring the reliability and security of the Platform and on our legal cybersecurity obligations.

Compliance with accounting, tax and legal obligations (evidentiary archiving of transaction records and response to lawful requests): processing based on compliance with a legal obligation.

Optional information communications: based exclusively on your prior consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

We never use your personal data for advertising profiling, and we neither sell nor rent your data to any third party.

Technical processors (within the meaning of Law No. 2024/017): we use technical providers acting exclusively on our instructions and subject to strict security and confidentiality commitments: Supabase (cloud hosting of the infrastructure and database, and account authentication), an e-mail service provider for sending login codes (currently Google, through the Gmail service) and Campay in connection with the approved operators MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money (secure processing of mobile payments).

Third-party service WhatsApp: when you tap an Advertiser’s WhatsApp contact button, the exchange opens in the WhatsApp application outside SHUYA’s infrastructure and is governed by WhatsApp’s own terms of use and privacy policy.

Public and judicial authorities: only upon regular legal or judicial request from the competent authorities of the Republic of Cameroon.

6. Cloud hosting and international data transfers

To ensure high availability and optimal security (encryption of data in transit using the TLS/HTTPS protocol), the Platform’s database is hosted on the secure cloud infrastructure of our processor Supabase, whose servers are located outside Cameroonian territory, in the United Kingdom (hosting region “eu-west-2”, London).

In accordance with Law No. 2024/017 of 23 December 2024 on the protection of personal data, these cross-border technical flows are governed by the security and confidentiality commitments of our processors and are strictly necessary for the performance of the contract binding you to SHUYA. By creating an account or using the Platform, you expressly consent to this secure technical hosting. SHUYA DIGITAL GROUP further undertakes to complete any declaration or prior authorisation formality required with the Personal Data Protection Authority (APDP), including for transfers of data outside Cameroon, in accordance with the regulatory procedures in force.

7. Data retention periods

User account data: kept as long as your account remains active, then deleted or anonymised after a period of twelve (12) months following your last login or your account deletion request.

Property listings: kept online during their active publication period, then archived in a restricted database for twelve (12) months from their withdrawal or unpublication for the purposes of moderation, fraud prevention and handling of any disputes.

Payment records and Pass receipts: kept for a mandatory statutory period of ten (10) years from the close of the relevant financial year, in accordance with the OHADA Uniform Act of 26 January 2017 on accounting law and financial reporting (AUDCIF).

Report files and security logs: kept for twenty-four (24) months from the closure of the handling of the report.

Supporting documents for verification: consulted solely for the verification requested for the award of the “Verified” badge and deleted once this verification has been carried out, unless retention is necessary in the event of a dispute.

8. Your rights over your personal data

In accordance with Law No. 2024/017 of 23 December 2024 on the protection of personal data, you have at any time a right of access to your data, a right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete information, a right to erasure (deletion), a right to restriction of processing, a right to object on legitimate grounds and a right to portability of your data in the cases provided for by law. To exercise your rights, write to shuyadigitalgroup@gmail.com stating your request and the e-mail address of your SHUYA account; we reply within a maximum of thirty (30) calendar days. The deletion of your account can be requested in the same way (Article 4.5 of the General Terms of Use). You may also refer the matter to the Personal Data Protection Authority (APDP).

9. Security measures and breach notification

In accordance with Law No. 2024/017 of 23 December 2024 and Law No. 2010/012 of 21 December 2010 on cybersecurity and cybercrime, we apply strict technical and organisational measures: HTTPS/TLS encryption, one-time login codes, masking of contact details without an active Pass, restricted and logged access control, and isolation of payment keys on our servers. In the event of a data breach likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the incident to the APDP and to the Users concerned in accordance with Law No. 2024/017.

10. Protection of minors

SHUYA is strictly reserved for persons aged at least eighteen (18) full years, under the conditions of legal capacity specified in Article 4.2 of the General Terms of Use. If a parent or guardian finds that a minor has created an account, they may write to shuyadigitalgroup@gmail.com to obtain its immediate deletion.
