Part B — Refund guarantee for the Pass

Scope: in force from 11 November 2026. Supplements Article 9 of the General Terms of Use.

1. Principle of the guarantee

If the Pass you purchased did not provide you with the access service announced, because of a technical failure or a fraudulent or outdated listing, you may request a full refund under the conditions defined below. This contractual guarantee applies without prejudice to the mandatory rights that Cameroonian legislation grants to consumers (in particular under Framework Law No. 2011/012 of 6 May 2011 and Law No. 2010/021 of 21 December 2010, within the conditions and limits set by those texts).

2. Cases giving right to a refund of the Pass

You are entitled to a full refund of your Pass if, within forty-eight (48) hours following confirmation of its purchase, you find and report to us one of the following situations:

False or misleading listing: a Listing you unlocked using the Pass turns out to be fictitious or fraudulent (non-existent property, Advertiser with no ownership right or mandate, or rent/price and substantial characteristics demanded by the Advertiser manifestly different from those published in the Listing);

Property already let or sold: the property referred to in the Listing consulted was already let, sold or unavailable while the Listing was still displayed as active on the Platform;

Advertiser persistently unreachable: the WhatsApp or telephone number displayed on the Listing is out of service or the Advertiser remains totally unreachable despite your documented attempts to make contact;

Technical payment or activation anomaly: your Pass was not activated in the application although your Mobile Money account was actually debited, or you were accidentally debited twice for the purchase of the same Pass.

3. Exclusions from the guarantee

The 48-hour commercial refund guarantee does not cover:

Mere personal change of mind: the fact that you no longer wish to look for housing or that the property visited does not match your subjective preferences after the visit, provided the Listing was accurate and the property available;

Failure of negotiations between parties: a commercial disagreement between you and the Advertiser concerning the negotiation of rent, sale price, number of months of advance, or lease clauses;

Sums paid directly to an Advertiser or a third party: SHUYA never collects rent, advance payments, deposits, viewing fees or sale prices and can in no case refund funds that you paid to a third party outside the purchase of the Pass. Never pay money before having visited the property and checked the titles and the identity of the person you are dealing with;

Fraudulent or abusive requests: repeated refund requests following massive consultation of the listings catalogue, or based on inaccurate statements.

4. Procedure for requesting a refund

From the application: open the page of the Listing concerned and tap the “Report a problem / request a refund” button;

Reason and supporting information: select the reason corresponding to your situation and briefly describe the facts observed (date and time of the call or WhatsApp message, reply received or anomaly noted);

By e-mail or WhatsApp: if you cannot use the application (in particular in case of double debit or non-activation of the Pass), write to us directly at shuyadigitalgroup@gmail.com or at +237 677 293 570 stating the e-mail address of your account and the Mobile Money / Campay transaction reference shown on your receipt.

5. Processing of the request and refund terms

Review period: our team processes your request within a maximum of three (3) working days from its receipt. If necessary, we may ask you for additional information or check the availability of the property with the Advertiser.

Full refund: when the request is approved, the refund is made for the full amount of the Pass paid (2,000 FCFA), by Mobile Money transfer (MTN MoMo or Orange Money) directly to the telephone number used for the initial payment, within a maximum of seven (7) working days.

Deactivation of the Pass: the execution of the refund entails the immediate deactivation of the refunded Pass.

Reasons in case of refusal: if the request does not meet the refund conditions, we will notify you in writing of the precise reasons for the refusal. You retain all the remedies provided for in Article 15 of the General Terms of Use.

Before payment, the application informs you of your fifteen (15)-day right of withdrawal and asks you to tick a box by which you expressly request the immediate performance of the digital service. SHUYA keeps a record of your request for immediate performance (date and time).
